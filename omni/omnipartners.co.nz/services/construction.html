Services

We’re end-to-end partners, providing smart advice and vital connections at any stage of your development. We work with you to realise the opportunities your property or potential acquisitions present.

  • Site finding
  • Feasibility
  • Design
  • Construction

Omni can help line you up with a construction partner you can trust, who will manage the building process and deliver a quality end result.

There is a lot to juggle in construction. You need someone you can trust who can manage the project efficiently, communicate clearly and be responsive when you need them to be.

Omni can assist you with the process of finding the right contractor, help you understand how to engage with them to get the best outcomes, and also give you advice on managing your construction team once they are on-site and work is underway.

We can also manage consent conditions to ensure a smooth transition at the end of the project into settlements.
